10. Methodology and limitations

  • Project policy: EARLY < -60 seconds; ON_TIME -60 through 300; LATE above 300 through 600; VERY_LATE above 600.
  • Calculated absolute-event-time delta is selected first; reported delay is the fallback.
  • Arrivals and departures use separate observations and denominators.
  • Canonical observations are selected deterministically from persisted reliability facts.
  • The public dashboard embeds persisted aggregates, compact histogram bins, and deterministic bounded stop/trip examples; complete event-level facts remain in DuckDB.
  • Matching requires complete persistence and static-snapshot lineage.
  • Reported cancellations include only explicit observed CANCELED relationships.
  • No passenger weighting, headway adherence, excess wait time, or composite index is included.
  • Manual or sparse captures demonstrate technical feasibility only.
